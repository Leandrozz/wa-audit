# Productos y temas consultados

Map WHAT clients ask about: products, services, and recurring topics — the
demand signal hidden in the channel.

Look for:
- Products/services by name or description, grouped into families. Count
  distinct threads per family, not raw mentions (one enthusiastic client can
  mention a product twenty times).
- Demand with no supply: things clients ask for that the business appears not
  to offer (or never answers about).
- Seasonality inside the active window, only if the window is long enough to
  support it — say so if it isn't.
- Topics that dominate volume vs topics that convert (cross-reference threads
  that end in an apparent order vs those that die).

Quote the client's own words for product names — their vocabulary is how they
search, and it rarely matches the catalog's naming.

Suggested row columns: product/topic family, distinct threads, example quote,
signal (demand, confusion, missed opportunity).
