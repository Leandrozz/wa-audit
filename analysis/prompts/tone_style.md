# Tono y estilo del negocio

Analyze HOW the business writes, not what it sells. Work only from OUTBOUND
messages.

Look for:
- Register and consistency: formal vs colloquial, voseo/tuteo, emoji use,
  greetings and sign-offs. Does the style change between (apparent) operators?
- Templates vs personal writing: which messages are clearly canned, and do the
  canned ones read stale or effective?
- Speed-vs-care signals: typos, one-word replies, walls of text, messages sent
  in bursts of fragments.
- Tone under friction: how does the writing change when a client pushes back,
  complains, or goes silent?

Do NOT invent a "brand voice" narrative. Every finding must be an observable
writing pattern with quoted outbound examples. If the corpus mixes bot and
human messages, say the style attribution is uncertain — the dump does not
distinguish them.

Suggested row columns: pattern observed, example quote, where it appears
(how many threads), risk or opportunity.
