# FATE · Emoción — del listado de specs a la conversación que se siente

Diagnostic lens inspired by the FATE model and the cortex/limbic communication
distinction in Chase Hughes' *The Behavior Ops Manual*, applied as an AUDIT of
the business's own communication. Original articulation; not affiliated with
or endorsed by the author.

Decisions are driven by emotion and rationalized with logic afterwards; a
conversation that only transfers information engages the weakest lever there
is. Analyze whether the business's messages ever reach the emotional layer —
honestly, without manufacturing pressure.

Examine, with counted evidence:

1. **Spec-dump vs felt-benefit ratio**: classify outbound persuasive sentences
   as information-transfer (specs, prices, logistics) versus emotion-directing
   (outcome imagery — "cuando la tengas instalada vas a...", empathy
   statements, brief client stories). A pitch that is 100% spec-list is
   bottom-heavy on the weakest lever; say so with numbers.
2. **State-response matching**: when an inbound message carries hesitation or
   worry ("no sé", "me da cosa", repeated questions about the same detail),
   does the reply address the STATE (reassurance, guarantee, simplification)
   or just repeat facts? Facts against feelings lose; count the mismatches.
3. **De-escalation delta**: for each angry/agitated inbound message, compare
   the emotional temperature before and after the business's reply within the
   thread. Did the reply cool it down (acknowledge, own, fix) or heat it up?
4. **Anticipation building**: does the business ever build concrete positive
   anticipation ("te aviso apenas salga del depósito así la seguís") versus
   only closing transactions? Anticipation of the payoff drives behavior more
   than the payoff itself.
5. **Emotional salience of critical facts**: are decision-critical details
   (price, deadline, next step) delivered with framing that makes them stick,
   or buried mid-paragraph in dry text the client will not retain?
6. **Manufactured emotion (ethics flag)**: fear/urgency manufactured without
   basis ("último disponible" repeated weekly) is manipulation, not emotional
   intelligence. If the corpus shows it, report it as a risk finding.

Suggested row columns: emotional pattern, evidence (threads + quote),
frequency, effect on the conversation, recommendation.
