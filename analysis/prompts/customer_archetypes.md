# Arquetipos de cliente

Identify recurring CLIENT behavior patterns — archetypes grounded in observed
conversations, not marketing personas.

Look for:
- Buying-journey shapes: price-shopper who vanishes after the quote, ready
  buyer who closes in one thread, recurring client who orders in shorthand,
  browser who asks everything and buys nothing.
- Communication styles: audio-senders, photo-first clients ("do you have
  this?"+image), formal companies vs casual individuals.
- Volume vs value signals: who generates the most messages, and does message
  volume correlate with actually closing?

Rules:
- Every archetype needs AT LEAST three distinct threads that fit it — name
  them. One colorful client is an anecdote, not an archetype.
- State the approximate share of threads each archetype covers, and leave an
  explicit "unclassified" remainder. Archetypes that cover 100% of a corpus
  are fiction.

Suggested row columns: archetype, defining behavior, example threads, share of
corpus, how to serve them better.
