# FATE · Autoridad — certeza, compostura y palabra cumplida

Diagnostic lens inspired by the FATE model and the authority-trait frameworks
in Chase Hughes' *The Behavior Ops Manual*, applied as an AUDIT of the
business's own communication. Original articulation; not affiliated with or
endorsed by the author.

In text, authority reduces to message craft: clients unconsciously read
certainty, consistency and composure from words alone, and grant or withhold
trust accordingly. Analyze OUTBOUND messages only.

Examine, with counted evidence:

1. **Hedging density**: "creo que", "capaz", "no estoy seguro", trailing
   qualifiers, unanswered deferrals — versus calm declarative answers on
   price, delivery and policy. Hedged answers on core questions erode trust.
2. **Collapse markers** (the pleasing extreme): apology spirals ("mil
   disculpas", "perdón la molestia"), minimizers ("solo para molestar"),
   unsolicited discounts BEFORE any client objection, instant folding on every
   pushback. This is the classic new-business failure mode with clients.
3. **Posturing markers** (the inflating extreme): bragging ("somos los #1"),
   status name-dropping, condescension. Both extremes read as insecurity; the
   target is composure: calm, direct, open agenda.
4. **Ownership vs deflection**: "me ocupo yo, te confirmo a las 15" versus
   "es política de la empresa", "el sistema no me deja", "no es mi área".
   Count responsibility-deflection phrases per thread.
5. **Promise-kept rate**: extract every outbound commitment with a stated time
   ("te lo mando hoy", "mañana te confirmo") and check IN THE THREAD whether it
   was met. Kept timelines are the most concrete trust signal text can carry.
6. **Boundary-setting**: does the business ever calmly set expectations
   (response windows, payment terms, scope) and decline out-of-scope demands
   with an alternative — or does it over-accommodate everything?
7. **Composure under fire**: after an angry or aggressive inbound message, does
   the reply stay level (acknowledge + concrete fix) or mirror the agitation /
   turn defensive? Never respond to an accusation by defending yourself is the
   standard; measure against it.

Suggested row columns: authority signal, evidence (threads + quote), frequency,
collapse/composure/posturing reading, recommendation.
