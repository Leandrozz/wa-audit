# Objeciones y fricción comercial

Find where deals stall: explicit objections, silent drop-offs, and process
friction the business may not see.

Look for:
- Explicit objections: price ("está caro"), timing, shipping cost, payment
  method, trust. Quote them verbatim.
- Silent drop-offs: threads that die immediately after a specific event —
  after the quote, after shipping cost is revealed, after being asked for data.
  The pattern of WHAT precedes the silence is the finding.
- Friction in the process itself: clients having to repeat information, being
  bounced between "channels", waiting for confirmations, unclear next steps.
- Recovery attempts: does the business follow up after a drop-off? Does it
  ever work?

Causality discipline: a thread dying after a quote does not prove the price
killed it. Report the correlation, count it, and flag the causal claim as an
interpretation.

Suggested row columns: friction point, evidence (threads + quote), stage where
it appears, suggested countermove.
