# Qué podría resolver un bot

Identify which parts of the conversation flow a bot or automation could handle
— and, just as important, which parts it must NOT touch.

Look for:
- High-volume, low-variance exchanges: same question, same answer, every time
  (hours, shipping zones, catalog requests, payment methods).
- The first-response gap: threads where a fast canned acknowledgment would have
  prevented a client from going cold (cross-check with unanswered threads).
- Hand-off moments: where automated flow must stop and a human must take over
  (negotiation, complaints, custom quotes).
- What already looks automated in the corpus, and whether clients respond well
  or badly to it.

Be conservative: recommend automation only where the corpus shows a repeated,
stable pattern. An automation recommendation built on two examples is refutable
and will be refuted.

Suggested row columns: opportunity, evidence in corpus (threads), what the bot
would do, human hand-off condition.
